How We Work

Getting started is straightforward. But the real value unfolds over time.

01

Discovery Call — We listen first.

A 30-minute conversation to understand your needs, your current workflow, your pain points, and what success looks like for you. We also share how we operate because fit matters to us. We choose our clients as carefully as they choose us, and we need to understand your vision, your goals, and your standards to know we can genuinely help. No pitch. No pressure. Just clarity.

02

Scope & Alignment — We define the engagement together.

Based on the discovery call, we propose a scope, timeline, and team structure tailored to your project. We align on deliverables, standards, tools, and communication rhythm before any work begins. Nothing starts until we both know exactly what success looks like.

03

Onboarding & Integration — We plug into your environment.

We adopt your templates, your BIM standards, your file structure, your collaboration platform. This integration period doesn’t mean we’re not productive — we deliver from day one. But we’re also learning your operation in parallel: absorbing your standards, understanding your expectations, mapping your workflows. We learn fast. We don’t ask the same question twice and if something’s unclear, we ask immediately rather than guess. The investment in onboarding pays off permanently, because once we’re truly embedded, we operate like part of your team.

04

Delivery & Optimization — We produce, we review, we improve.

Every deliverable flows through our internal QA process before it reaches you. But we don’t stop at delivery. As the engagement matures, we optimize — faster turnarounds, fewer iterations, better anticipation of your needs. We track our own performance and actively push for improvement, project after project.

05

Growth & Development — We push things forward.

This is where we go beyond what you asked for. Once we understand your practice deeply, we start seeing opportunities you haven’t had time to explore — workflow automations, process improvements, R&D solutions, tools that didn’t exist before. We don’t just give you peace of mind on production. We become a development engine for your practice.

RAMI KARA MOSTEFA

Founder & CEO — Licensed Architect, BIM Strategist, MBA

Rami Kara Mostefa is the founder and CEO of RKM Studio. A second-generation architect, he trained at EPAU Algiers, earned a Master's in BIM Management from Zigurat, and an MBA from Quantic. He founded RKM on a simple premise: that AEC firms deserve a partner that thinks like an architect, operates like an insider, and cares like an owner. He leads the studio with the conviction that great work only happens when trust, excellence, and genuine commitment come together.

The Vision

I grew up in a family of architects. Both my parents built their careers in this industry — so I didn't just study architecture, I was raised in it. Blueprints on the dining table, site visits on weekends, conversations about structure and materials before I knew what any of it meant. There was never a question of what I'd become — the question was what kind of architect I'd be.

I trained at EPAU in Algiers, graduated with the Excellence Prize, and went straight into the deep end — entrepreneurship, founding a BIM company, and working with international teams while still in my twenties. Along the way, I earned a Master's in Global BIM Management from Zigurat and an MBA from Quantic School of Business & Technology, because I realized early that building great models isn't the same as building a great company.

But my first venture taught me something no degree could. I watched partnerships dissolve, trust break down, and good work go unrecognized — not because the quality wasn't there, but because reliability, communication, and genuine care were missing. Technical excellence alone doesn't build lasting relationships. What builds them is trust, consistency, and the willingness to care about someone else's project as if it were your own. That lesson cost me. But it's also what made everything that came after possible.

RKM Studio was born from that conviction. Not just a BIM company — but a practice that combines architectural and engineering expertise with the technological rigor and adaptability that today's industry demands. We're also a licensed architecture firm in Algeria, designing and delivering real projects — which means our technical services are grounded in firsthand experience of what it takes to bring a building to life.

Every decision I make — how we hire, how we onboard clients, how we deliver, how we communicate — is shaped by one question: would I trust us if I were on the other side? That's why we're selective about who we work with — and that applies to our team as much as our clients. That's why we invest deeply before we expect anything in return. And that's why, after five years and over 400 projects, no client has ever left.
